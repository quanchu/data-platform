# A week across the company

## Energy operations

The solar division increased production this quarter. Engineers installed new panels at the northern site. The team expects higher output next month.

Solar demand continued to grow. The division hired additional technicians and expanded maintenance coverage. Its latest report forecasts further growth.

The weekly briefing covered several independent updates:

- The solar division opened a new site in Hà Nội.
- The museum acquired a dinosaur skeleton.
  - The exhibit includes a reconstruction of its habitat.
- The football club signed a goalkeeper.

  The player will join training next week.

## Transport operations

The transport division introduced electric buses. Drivers completed training on the new vehicles. The city plans to expand routes next year.

The new buses support longer routes and quieter travel. Operators will monitor battery performance during the first month. Passengers can use existing tickets.
